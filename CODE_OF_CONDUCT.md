# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner, Neeraj Gupta, through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and addressed in a manner appropriate to the circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The maintainer may use correction, warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal depending on the impact and persistence of the behavior.
